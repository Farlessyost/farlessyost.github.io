01 / DOORWAY

Motion across the opening

No motion reported

Mount the sensor on the fixed jamb and aim the hood across the opening. A person crossing the field of view triggers a motion event. One PIR cannot tell which way they went.

02 / DOOR

Door open or closed

Door closed: magnet beside sensor

Fit the powered sensor to the frame on the latch side, with the magnet beside it on the door. Opening the door separates them and changes the reed-switch state.

03 / AT THE COMPUTER

Receiver and activity log

The receiver sits beside the computer and gets power through USB. It passes sensor events to the logger, which adds timestamps and saves them in SQLite. The OLED lets you check sensor activity at the receiver.
